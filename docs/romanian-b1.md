# Romanian B1 in Sprigo

The Romanian course has an A1 path and a B1 path. The B1 path opens after you
pass the A1 checkpoint or the A1 level test. If you passed A1 before B1 was
added, Sprigo opens the new path using your saved checkpoint. The first two
units review frequent A2 structures and extend them into B1 narration and planning.
If you still struggle with basic past and future forms, use the A1–A2 resource below.

## Study routine

Work through each unit's four lessons in Learn. Use the unit guidebook to
revisit examples and Practice to review mistakes and recall vocabulary.
Open the matching unit in Stories for reading practice. For feedback on a
short composition, choose the unit in Practice → Writing. In Roleplay,
rehearse the scenario from the table and respond aloud where voice input is
available. The app generates its exercises and feedback using your configured AI model.

For listening practice, hear a recording once for the main point and again
for details. Summarize it in two or three Romanian sentences before checking
the answers or transcript. Use the linked UBB and ILR audio for practice with
recorded voices.

| Unit | Language focus | Independent practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Telling Stories | Compound past, imperfect and pluperfect | Tell a friend how you missed a train. Give the background, what happened next and what had already happened. |
| 2. Making Plans | Future forms, subjunctive and time clauses | Reschedule an appointment. Give a reason, propose two new times and confirm one. |
| 3. Travel and Everyday Services | Object pronouns and the polite conditional | After a cancellation, ask for an alternative. Confirm the departure time, platform and cost. |
| 4. Housing and the Neighborhood | Comparisons, relative clauses and possession | Compare two rentals, then write to a landlord about a fault and arrange access for a repair. |
| 5. Work and Learning | Duration, instructions and reported speech | Relay a colleague's message and send a follow-up email with a clear deadline. |
| 6. Health and Habits | Advice, obligation and reflexive verbs | In a fictional appointment, give a timeline of symptoms and check what documents to bring next time. |
| 7. Media and Culture | Attribution, uncertainty and contrast | Summarize a short local report and distinguish what happened from what a source thinks. |
| 8. Community and the Environment | Cause, purpose, impersonal constructions and conditions | Summarize a community notice and propose one action with an owner and deadline. |
| 9. Discussing Choices | Reasons, concessions and compromises | Discuss two plans, defend your preference and agree on a fallback. |
| 10. Using Romanian Independently | Listening, reading, writing and interaction | Complete the four tasks below before taking the B1 checkpoint. |

## End-of-course practice

The Sprigo checkpoint has a target score of 80%. Use these tasks to check
skills that need an extended answer, and compare your performance against
the published ILR sample.

- **Listening.** Use an ILR B1 audio task. Identify the main point on the first
  play and note the details the task asks for on the second.
- **Reading.** Use a B1 notice, email or article from the ILR sample.
  Explain its purpose and relay two details to someone who has not read it.
- **Writing.** Write a 100–120 word email about a course that was rescheduled.
  Explain the problem, propose a new date and ask for written confirmation.
  Check that someone could act on the message after reading it once.
- **Speaking.** Rehearse a two-minute conversation about a cancelled booking.
  Explain why you are calling, ask a follow-up question, discuss an alternative
  and confirm the agreement. Check that your reasons and next step are clear.

## Books and practice materials

- **Council of Europe, CEFR Companion Volume (2020).** The B1 descriptors for
  reception, production, interaction and mediation inform the course goals.
  [CEFR descriptors](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/cefr-descriptors)
- **Ioana Sonea, Lavinia-Iunia Vasiu and Dina Vîlcu, _Manual de limba română ca limbă
  străină (RLS), nivelul B1_ (2021).** Main companion textbook with model texts,
  grammar, recorded material and an answer key. The publisher's free preview
  shows the syllabus and types of activities.
  [Publisher](https://libraria.ubbcluj.ro/produs/manual-de-limba-straina-ca-limba-straina-nivelul-b1/) ·
  [Preview](https://libraria.ubbcluj.ro/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Rezumat-Limba-romana-pentru-straini-nivel-B1.pdf)
- **Elena Platon, Ioana Sonea, Lavinia-Iunia Vasiu and Dina Vîlcu,
  _Descrierea minimală a limbii române_.** Romanian-specific reference for
  choosing functions, structures and vocabulary by level.
  [Publisher](https://libraria.ubbcluj.ro/produs/descrierea-minimala-a-limbii-romane/)
- **Elena Platon, Ioana Sonea and Dina Vîlcu, _Manual de limba română ca limbă
  străină (RLS) A1–A2_.** Review alongside units 1 and 2 if you are starting
  immediately after Sprigo A1. The publisher also provides the audio.
  [Publisher and audio](https://libraria.ubbcluj.ro/produs/manual-de-limba-romana-ca-limba-straina-rls-a1-a2/)
- **Babeș-Bolyai University, RLNM audio exercises.** Choose the B1 column
  for travel, housing, health, city life or leisure.
  [Audio and worksheets](https://video.elearning.ubbcluj.ro/?page_id=984)
- **Institutul Limbii Române, Romanian proficiency sample tests.** Use the
  B1 section of the B1/B2 download, with its matching audio, for independent
  practice after unit 10.
  [Sample tests and recordings](https://www.ilr.ro/teste-pentru-verificarea-cunostintelor-de-limba-ro/)
- **Babeș-Bolyai University, Centrul Alpha Romanian sample test.** The PDF
  and MP3 are accessible directly for additional practice with a longer test.
  [Sample PDF](https://lett.ubbcluj.ro/alpha/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/RO-2018.pdf) ·
  [Listening MP3](https://lett.ubbcluj.ro/alpha/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Bicicleta.mp3)
